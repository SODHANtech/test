# India's Lithium Paradox: Why Mining Isn't Enough

## Verified Facts & Sources
- **Resource in Reasi (Jammu & Kashmir):** In 2023, the Geological Survey of India (GSI) established an inferred (G3 stage) resource of 5.9 million tonnes of lithium-bearing aluminous laterite/bauxite ore with an average grade of ~583 ppm Li. It is *not* 5.9 million tonnes of pure metallic lithium.
- **Processing Reality:** Raw ore cannot be scooped directly into a battery. It must undergo benefication, high-temperature treatment / calcination or acid leaching, and hydrometallurgical purification to produce battery-grade lithium carbonate or lithium hydroxide (>99.5% purity).
- **Global Supply Chain Asymmetry:**
  - Lithium chemical refining: Dominated by China (~65% global capacity).
  - Nickel refining: Dominated by Indonesia (~37-43% global capacity).
  - Mining location ≠ refining power.
- **Cabinet Scheme (November 4, 2026):**
  - ₹2,814 crore outlay approved under the National Critical Minerals Mission.
  - 30% capital subsidy to set up 4 domestic processing plants:
    - 2 for lithium (minimum 30 kt capacity each)
    - 2 for nickel (minimum 50 kt capacity each).
